# Authorship

Shoddy — the language design, specification, documentation, standard
library (the machines), toolchain (the mill), tests, website, name, and
tagline — was conceived, designed, directed, curated, and is owned by
**Stephen Vincent Foster**. Copyright is claimed by the author and
licensed to the public under the [MIT License](LICENSE).

## Disclosure

Shoddy was developed with **Claude (Anthropic)**, an AI coding tool,
used as a drafting instrument: it produced code and prose drafts at the
author's direction, within constraints the author set, subject to the
author's acceptance, rejection, and revision — alongside the other
tools of the trade (GCC, the .NET SDK and Roslyn, VS Code, pandoc,
GitHub Actions). Every design decision in the language was a human
decision: what Shoddy would be, what it would refuse to be, what
entered it, what stayed out, what it was called, and in what order it
was built. The development conversation is preserved verbatim
(the author's messages typos-and-all) in the project's
[DEVLOG](https://shoddymills.github.io/shoddy/devlog.html) as a
contemporaneous record of that creative control.

This position is consistent with current U.S. law: *Thaler v.
Perlmutter*, No. 23-5233 (D.C. Cir. 2025), *cert. denied* (2026),
holds that machines cannot be authors — a case about autonomous
generation, not about humans using tools — and the U.S. Copyright
Office's *Copyright and Artificial Intelligence, Part 2:
Copyrightability* (Jan. 2025) confirms that using AI as an assistive
tool does not limit protection, and that human authorship attaches
through creative direction, selection, arrangement, and revision of
machine-drafted material.

The abstract rules of the language — the grammar and semantics — are a
method of operation (17 U.S.C. § 102(b)) that anyone is free to
implement. The claim here is to the expression: the documents, the
source, the site, and the record.

The full statement, with the development record and legal analysis, is
on the documentation site:
**[Authorship](https://shoddymills.github.io/shoddy/authorship.html)**.
