Summary

seedfin bridges fin's rates, time value of money, growth, statistics, budgeting, loan amortization and payoff, NPV/IRR/payback, CAGR/ROI, dollar-cost averaging, margin and markup, break-even, payroll and depreciation. Per R4.16 there is no RECORD cell at the keyboard: every Fin* record fin.shoddy returns is a dict here, the same LIST-of-PAIR shape seeddict already established, one entry per field.

Several words pre-flight a condition fin.shoddy itself Errors on rather than let it abort: FINNPER and, through it, FINCARDPAYOFF/FINEXTRAPAYOFF, refuse a payment that never clears the interest; FINIRR refuses when the cash-flow bracket shows no sign change; FINMIRR refuses with no negative cash flow to finance; FINCARDMINPAYOFF refuses a minimum rate at or below the periodic interest rate, which makes non-payoff certain rather than merely possible.

Not bridged: FinDate and everything built on it — FinSerial, FinFromSerial, FinDateAdd, FinDaysInMonth, FinDateAddMonths, FinDays360, FinDateDiff, FinYearFrac, FinCouponsBefore, FinLastCoupon, FinAccrued, FinBondPrice, FinYtm and FinMdur. That is a whole second subsystem — a calendar and a bond pricer built on it — coherent enough to deserve its own pass.
