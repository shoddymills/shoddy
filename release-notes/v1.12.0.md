Least squares at the keyboard, with the model given as a word rather than
a number to remember — and a set of dictionary words renamed so that a
session which knows the machine can find the word without a translation
table.

## Breaking

**Nine reckoner dictionary words are renamed, and the old spellings are
gone rather than aliased.** A tape or session that names one of them will
now fail with an unknown word, which is the intended outcome: two
spellings of one idea is the thing seedbuiltin's own rules exist to
prevent.

- **`STDEV` → `STDDEV`, `STDEVP` → `STDDEVP`** in
  [seedstats](https://shoddymills.github.io/shoddy/machines/seedstats.html).
  `stats.shoddy` declares `StdDev` and `StdDevP`, so the one-D spelling
  was the single place in the tree where a dictionary word could not be
  found by searching the machine it came from — and `seedfin` and
  `seedeng` already spelled their own twins `FINSTDDEV` and `ENGSTDDEV`,
  so the tree carried two spellings of one idea.
- **Seven words in each of
  [seedeng](https://shoddymills.github.io/shoddy/machines/seedeng.html)
  and [seedfin](https://shoddymills.github.io/shoddy/machines/seedfin.html)
  lose an extra `S`**: `ENGSMEAN`/`FINSMEAN` → `ENGMEAN`/`FINMEAN`, and
  likewise `SVAR` → `VAR`, `SMEDIAN` → `MEDIAN`, `SQUANTILE` →
  `QUANTILE`, `SMIN` → `MINIMUM`, `SMAX` → `MAXIMUM`, `SRANGE` →
  `RANGEOF`. The `S` disambiguated against the bare `MEAN` and `MIN` — a
  collision the `ENG`/`FIN` prefix had already prevented — and it was
  applied unevenly anyway: `FINSVAR` sat beside `FINVARP`, and
  `FINCORREL` never took the `S` though `CORREL` is taken too. The name
  is now the machine's own, uppercased, and nothing added.

## Added

- **`ENGFITOF` and `FINFITOF`** — least squares through two columns,
  answering a dict of intercept, slope and `rfit`. The `rfit` is the
  correlation in the **fitted** space, which is the R every spreadsheet
  reports.
- **The four models are words**: `ENGLINEAR`, `ENGLOGMODEL`,
  `ENGEXPMODEL`, `ENGPOWERMODEL`, and the `FIN` set beside them. This is
  the choice `seedfin` already made for its day-count bases — a session
  reads `FINLOGMODEL` rather than `2`, and a mistyped model is an
  unknown word instead of a different curve fitted in silence. The two
  seeds are written the same way on purpose: a session that has learned
  one has learned the other.
- **Five guards on each.** Four are conditions `eng.shoddy` and
  `fin.shoddy` already `Error` on by name — the three model domains (a
  logarithm of a non-positive x, an exponential of a non-positive y, a
  power model needing both) and a model neither recognises. The fifth is
  one neither machine states: `LinFit` divides by the variance of the x
  column and `Correl` by the product of the two standard deviations, so
  a column that does not vary divides by zero and **ends the run**. The
  test is exact equality of every element rather than a tolerance,
  because that is precisely when those denominators are exactly zero — a
  nearly-constant column gives a very large slope, which is the
  machine's answer and not the seed's to overrule. One such test covers
  all four models, the logarithm being injective.
- **A length check on the pair, though nothing aborts on a mismatch** —
  which is why it is needed. `Cov` zips the two lists and the zip stops
  at the shorter, so a mismatched pair would otherwise answer a fit of
  the overlap against the means of the whole: a wrong number rather than
  a refusal, and a wrong number is the failure this seed layer exists to
  prevent.
