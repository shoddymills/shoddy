## Fixed

- **The perch's two-million-frame test no longer fails on a slow run.** The
  test's debugger client gave up after 120 seconds of silence on the
  socket, and the deepest debug-woven recursion takes about 90 seconds on
  an idle machine and longer on a busy one. It now waits while the mill
  process is alive, as the other three depth tests do, and fails only when
  the mill exits without answering or sends nothing for ten minutes.
- **For contributors:** an editor restoring the two project templates in a
  checkout no longer leaves a `packages.lock.json` beside each one. The
  templates now have their own `Directory.Build.props`, which stops the
  repository's build properties from reaching them, as a scaffolded project
  never had them.
