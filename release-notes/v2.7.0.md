## Changed

- The documentation, error messages and lint messages are rewritten in
  plain language.
- The capability lint now reports a capability a `mill.manifest` declares
  but nothing in the mill reaches.

## Fixed

- Two projects in one solution that embed the same Mode T mill could fail
  the build with MSB3246 ("bad image … being used by another process"):
  one project's weave rewrote the DLL while the other was reading it. The
  weave now writes beside and renames into place, and leaves identical
  output untouched.
- A build could hang indefinitely behind a stuck `mill` process. The mill
  now waits at most ten minutes for another mill working on the same
  target, then fails with a message naming it.
